01 / About

Connecting the digital
and the physical.

I am a PhD Research Fellow at Østfold University of Applied Sciences (Høgskolen i Østfold), in the Digitalisation and Society programme.

My academic interests include cyber-physical systems, the Internet of Things, modelling physical processes, machine learning, and digital twins for industrial applications and collaboration.

DarTwin made precise by SysMLv2 – An Experiment

Øystein Haugen, Stefan Klikovits, Martin Arthur Andersen, Jonathan Beaulieu, Francis Bordeleau, Joachim Denil, and Joost Mertens

SAM 2025 · ACM/IEEE MODELS Companion · pp. 322–332

An investigation of how SysML v2 can express the DarTwin notation as a domain-specific language for digital twin evolution. The paper examines two case studies and the capabilities and limitations of the available graphical tooling.
